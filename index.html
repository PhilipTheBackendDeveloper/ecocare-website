About Our Company

EcoCare Cleaning Services Ltd (herein referred to as Ecoclean) Is a Startup Company formed and registered under the Ghanaian Companies Code 1963 on September 19th, 2014 to commence business on September 30th, 2014. The current shareholders of the Company are Eric Nana Acquah and Margaret Owusua Boakye (hereinafter referred to as “promoters”).


The Company has renowned and experienced environmentalist, fumigation and cleaning consultants with operation across Ghana and Main office at Achimota Mile 7 Accra. The company is a member of Contract Cleaners Association of Ghana (CCAG).
