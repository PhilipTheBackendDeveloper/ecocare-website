Service Details

EcoCare owns some of the world’s famous cleaning equipment. Our Carpet and upholstery machines work on the same principle as a washing machine effecting deep into the fibres of the carpet and upholstery. With our cleaning machines especially formulated detergent designed to loosen accumulated dirt from the carpet piles and chair fabrics. Detergents are mixed with warm water and sprayed into the carpets and chairs using the similar equipment, in the process the dirt is loose as a result of the reaction of this detergent and extracted from the chairs and carpet. Unlike other conventional cleaners, our equipment are designed to extract all the dirt including that which has found its way deep down in the carpet or upholstery due to lack of proper equipment. The extraction ensures that the carpet and upholstery is 90% dry preventing dampness, smell and staining.

At EcoCare, we understand that your upholstered furniture plays a vital role in your home or office, providing comfort, elegance, and functionality. Over time, however, daily use can lead to dirt, stains, and allergens embedding themselves into the fabric, making it look worn and reducing its lifespan. Our upholstery services are designed to breathe new life into your furniture, ensuring it remains clean, fresh, and inviting while extending its durability.

The process begins with a detailed inspection of your furniture to identify the type of fabric and assess its condition. This ensures we use the right cleaning method for the best results. We then prepare the surface by vacuuming away dirt and dust, focusing on the fabric and crevices where debris tends to accumulate. For tougher stains, we employ eco-friendly, fabric-safe solutions to gently lift them without causing damage.

Our deep cleaning methods include steam cleaning, which penetrates the fibers to remove dirt, bacteria, and allergens, or dry cleaning for more delicate fabrics. This not only restores the furniture’s original look but also contributes to a healthier indoor environment. For leather furniture, we go a step further, applying premium conditioners that restore softness, prevent cracking, and enhance the natural shine.

To keep your furniture protected, we offer fabric treatments that create a barrier against future stains and wear, ensuring that your investment lasts longer. Once the cleaning process is complete, we finish with a refreshing deodorizer to leave your furniture smelling as good as it looks. Before we leave, our team conducts a final inspection to make sure everything meets the highest standards of quality.

Choosing EcoCare means choosing excellence. We combine advanced cleaning techniques with environmentally friendly products to deliver unmatched results. Whether it’s your favorite sofa, office chairs, or even leather recliners, we provide comprehensive care that revitalizes your furniture and uplifts your space. At EcoCare, we take pride in helping you create a clean and welcoming environment with furniture that looks and feels brand new.
